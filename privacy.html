Privacy Policy — Joby Backup

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Who uses this app

Joby Backup is used only by its owner, Bryan, for personal backups. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests a single Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the app can create, read and delete only the files it uploaded itself. It cannot see any other file in the Google Drive.

How the data is used

The app stores encrypted backup files from the owner's own computer in the owner's own Google Drive, and reads them back when a restore is needed. Files are encrypted before they leave the computer.

Collection and sharing

The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing is stored anywhere other than the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files can be deleted from Google Drive like any other file.
